About

FIG. 01 — IN THE LAB

I am a Mechanical Engineer specializing in mechatronics from the University of British Columbia, with experience designing electromechanical systems across hydrogen humidifiers, energy hardware, automation, medical devices, nuclear, and research.

My work spans product and mechanical design, programming, manufacturing, rapid prototyping, and testing.

I enjoy tinkering at home and reverse engineer everyday items and try and justify design choices. My approach is to learn from first principles and apply it to my own designs.
